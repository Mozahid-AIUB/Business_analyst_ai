Who is using the platform, and for what

Every account, every sign-in, every file scanned and every health score run — with the rows processed, the exposure flagged and the trend over the last month. This console is separate from the customer application at index.html and is reachable only with a staff account.

The console reads the same account and usage store the customer application writes to. It runs no models of its own — every number here is counted from recorded events, not estimated.
